## How each piece behaves (the facts the tests pin)

**Council options (§6.5 step 1).** Target provinces = within 2 provinces of at least 3 of the nation's holdings (one entry per village, from the owners index; brute-force equality test). Per province: `strike` = the largest stack of ONE other nation (resident combat hosts, non-site tile; ties by smaller tile then nation), `value` = its troops; `camp` = a live camp, `value` = floor(troops / 2) (camps count whole troops); `raid` = the village of another nation with the most garrison + resident troops whose shield has ended by S, `value` = floor(0.8 x total). `own` = the sum of the nation's 4 largest resident combat hosts within 2 provinces of the target. An option is offered only if `own >= 1.5 x value`; ranked by value (ties `(p, q)`, then strike < camp < raid), one per province, top 3, numbered 1..n. The ratio word compares `own` with the WHOLE enemy (stack, camp, garrison + residents), so a camp can be offered and read "unfavourable" (own 330 against a camp of 400: value 200, 330 >= 300). `candidates_hash` / `options_hash` are the council store's own functions (`options_hash` is unchanged when value, own or ratio change: tested). Files read: the immutable `/h/province/{p},{q}/{C0 - 2}` through `feed.provinceAt`. During the first bell of the period a file that is not served yet is waited for; from C0 + 1 the newest served file within 4 bells is accepted and counted (`meta.fallback`). A nation's council opens only if every AI of the nation holds a final village at C0 (counted `skipped:not_all_final`, final for the period). `eligible` (wallets of the nation's citizens with a final holding, rule bots excluded) and `humans` (the non-AI ones: the seat) are fixed at C0 and handed to `council.open`.

**Council calls.** At C0 (or when it opens) each AI member of the nation is asked once for its motion (`mind.councilCall`, deadline `bell_start(C0+3) - margin`) and at C0 + 3 once for its ballot (deadline `bell_start(C0+6) - margin`). The asked set is persisted in `STATE/watcher/state.json`; a failed call is asked once more after 5 s and then given up; the mind caches a `not_ready` or `feed_lag` answer per (index, kind, period), so those are final for the period.

**Strike Order (§6.5 steps 6, 7).** At the close the council store calls `fixCall` -> `closeCall(f, k)`: tile from the files of bell C0 + 5 (strike: the largest enemy stack, camp: the camp tile, raid: the village tile; the tile at C0 - 2 is the hint when the file shows nothing), invited = the nation's resident combat hosts within 2 provinces of the target ordered (1) owners whose ballot chose the winner, (2) troops desc, ties by host id (numeric), first six. If those files are not served yet (the file of bell b appears about two bells after b) the Call stays unsealed and `sealPending` retries every second until S - 1; three bells past the file bell a province still missing counts as absent. At S + 2 the council store opens the Call; the watcher writes `result` = `{present, present_hosts, bounced, bounced_hosts, displaced, displaced_hosts, clash}` into the council file once the feed is complete through S + 2 (REVEAL rows with `arrive == S` in the Call's province for the nation; `clash` = `{p, q, tile, bell, engagements (the record's count), fighters_on_tile, engaged_on_tile, lost: {nation: troops}}` from the clash report, or null when no CLASH row exists). The opened Call feeds AC2's producer: with a closed council file the pump (`socialEpisodeSource`) now yields the `strike` episode and the +2 `adopted_mover` delta (test over AC4's real store and AC2's `episodes_from_events`, 6.5 / R4: the delta is dated S + 2, the close episode names no target).

**Release job (§7.2, R10).** A sealed record opens when the REVEAL of each of its marches is public AND that march's real arrival bell (from the DEPART) has ended (`job bell > arrive`), never before `release_bell` (S + 2 for a decision under a Strike Order); at most 6 bells are waited, counted from the end of `release_bell` (the job of bell `release_bell + 7` gives up) and a march nobody revealed opens as `destination: "unrevealed"`; a march the outcome shows was not sent opens at `release_bell` as `not_sent`. `PUB/open/<bell>.json` = `{v:1, bell, records:[opened...]}` is written once per bell with every record due at that bell; a record that becomes due later in the same bell waits for the next bell (the file is served immutable); a restart finds the file and only marks its records. Each opened record is AC1a's `records.open(id)` plus `{v, kind, by, wake, opened_bell, destinations:[{host_id, state: "revealed"|"unrevealed"|"not_sent", p, q, tile, planned_arrive_bell, arrive_bell, depart_bell, via, destination?}]}`: the real arrival bell next to the planned one, the destination from the public REVEAL (never from model text). The opened record opens its commit (tested with the same formula `records.mjs` uses). `PUB/open/index.json` = `{v:1, bells:[...], latest}` is an addition of mine so the page can find the files (not immutable, `max-age=2`).

**Wakes (§3.2).** `wakeEvents(tag, bell)` = the feed's W-CLASH / W-THREAT (the wave-A logic, reused) + W-DM (a direct message to the AI, once per sender per 6 bells), W-HALL (a nation message naming the AI in EN or JA, or a motion in its nation) and W-CALL (an adopted, sealed Strike Order of its nation, while `[follow_from, S - 1]`). Each wake is delivered once; a repeated (tag, bell) answers the same list; the gate's social cap (2) still applies.

**Chronicle (§6.7).** `motion`, `call_adopted` (nation, tally wording, S; no target, no option), `call_declined` (from the mind's event; `by: model`), `no_call` (with the reason in words), `strike_result` (at S + 2, with the target, present armies and losses per nation), `ai_joined` (public JOIN row matched with the roster, also when the roster arrives later), `ai_card_changed` (a new self-summary or a goal changing state), `ai_march_opened` (model marches only; an autopilot Strike-Order follow gets none). Tally wording: "with 1 AI ballot and the presenter's ballot" (origin 0 seat) or "... and a scripted seat ballot" (origin 2). Every line has `{id, kind, bell, day, actors, ai[], by?, refs, text:{en, ja}}`; `ai[]` from the roster only; lines are deduplicated by (kind, bell, actors, refs) and journaled. A sentinel test shows a motion's message text never reaches a chronicle line.

**Cards (§2.4).** One file per AI, rewritten only when its content changes (`updated_bell` = the bell of the change). `revealed_reasons` = the last 5 PUBLISHED reasons of model decisions: unsealed at once, sealed only after the release; each carries the cited episodes' code text, bell and age in bells from the episode store (a redacted episode drops out). `stats.model_marches_opened` counts model-chosen marches whose destination is public. `budget` from the persona cap and the day's counters.

**Events (§8.3).** `PUB/events/latest.json` = `{v:1, bell, from_bell, events:[...]}`, newest first, last 72 bells, at most 150: `{kind:"depart", bell, host_id, arrive_bell, mass (whole troops), origin:{p,q}, actor:{tag, kind: "ai"|"seat"|"script"|null, ai, faction, name:{en,ja}}, destination: null | {p,q,tile}}` (the destination only once the REVEAL is public) and `{kind:"clash", bell (clash bell), log_bell, p, q, engagements, arrivals, actors:[{...actor, arrived, engaged, fate, troops_after}]}` for real clashes (arrival or engagement) and only the actors that took part. Built from public rows only; the shape beyond "departures and clashes with actor tags and kind/ai flags" is mine, AC7 should read this file as written.

## Deviations from the contract (read these)

1. **Social wakes are the watcher's.** §3.2 lists W-DM, W-HALL, W-CALL with source "social" and AC1a's notes expect the watcher to carry them; the wave-A stub carried feed wakes only. They are built here (`wakes.mjs`).
2. **Council calls could not reach the brain: a seam between AC1a and AC3b, bridged here, not solved at the root.** `mind.councilCall` returns the TALK or BALLOT object, but the Rust brain signs and posts only what a `/v1/decide` answer carries in `social`, under THAT answer's `decision_id` (`follow.rs` `post_social`). Without a bridge no AI motion or ballot would ever be posted and the council beat (the owner's second beat) could not happen. `watcher/outbox.mjs` keeps each council call's output and `decorateAnswer` (wired in `server.mjs` around `mind.decide`) attaches it to the NEXT decide answer of the same AI (about one bell later: a motion is valid for 1 bell, a ballot for 2; expired ones are counted `outbox_expired`), numbering the motion after the answer's `say` items, and registers the item's provenance under that answer's record (a copy of the council record's own expectation; it reaches only in-memory `priv.social`, so it is lost on a restart and not journaled). The item JSON also carries the council decision's own `decision_id`. Proved end to end with AC4's real store and a brain stand-in (the service test). **The clean fix is in the brain** (`post_social` using an item's own `decision_id` when present), a Rust change in AC3b's file that is outside this unit; with it the piggyback can be deleted. M8's "provenance matches its decision's signed-ready output" (AC8) should read the session record's `priv.social` for these items.
3. **`release_bell` in the code is the planned arrival + 1; v1.3 §7.2 says the planned arrival.** AC1a's `api.mjs` commits `arrive + 1`. The job does not depend on it: it opens on the REVEAL condition and never before the committed `release_bell`, so both readings give an opening within 2 bells of M3's due bell (AC8 should take due = the later of `release_bell` and the real arrival bell, as written). Not changed here (AC1a's file).
4. **A Strike Order stayed "live" for the mind after its opening.** AC4's `memberCall` keeps answering after S + 2 (`opened: true`), `views.memberView` treated it as live, so every decision (and every `say`) of the nation stayed sealed until the next period replaced it. `server.mjs` now hands the views a `memberCall` that answers null once the Call is opened (test: a decision at S + 3 is not sealed; with the wrapper removed the test fails). A cleaner home would be `views.mjs` (AC1a).
5. **`closeCall` can return null at the close.** The files of bell C0 + 5 appear about two bells later; the contract has the Call sealed at the close. The Call is sealed when the files are served (usually at C0 + 7 or C0 + 8), through `sealPending`; members can read it from then, S = C0 + 12 leaves 4 bells.
6. **Strict files with a one-bell wait, then a flagged fallback** (see above): the contract says the files of bell C0 - 2; a council may open one bell late and, from C0 + 1, from the newest served file within 4 bells. The meta of each attempt records `fallback` and `missing`.
7. **Reading of the candidate rules where the contract is short.** `own` counts only the provinces fetched (the ball of radius 2 around each viable target); the raid check is the TARGET's shield at S only (the sender's shield is not checked; `policy::shield_refuses` is not ported); the raid village is the one with the largest garrison + residents; the strike stack is per nation; values are rounded down to whole troops; a host counts as resident when its entry state is the roster state and as combat when its unit is not the scout.
8. **Result at the first bell the log is complete through S + 2**, once; a CLASH row that arrives later does not update it (the `strike` episode still waits until S + 14 for a late row, AC2's rule).
9. **Extra files and fields not named by the contract:** `PUB/open/index.json`, the `events/latest.json` shape, `census.mjs`, `wakes.mjs`, `outbox.mjs`, `watcher` in `/v1/health`, `council_calls_known` in the counters.
10. **`createWatcher`'s `aiDir`** is AI_DIR (the wave-A call passed `PUB` as `aiDir`; §11.6 says `aiDir`, §8.3 says serve takes AI_DIR). `pubDir` / `stateDir` override.
