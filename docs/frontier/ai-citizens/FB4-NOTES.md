## 3. Deviations (the contract is not edited; the architect may want a v1.4 line)

1. **The option shape is extended.** §6.5 step 1 and the §8.3 table list `candidates:[{option, kind, p, q, value, own, ratio}]`; every option now also carries `enemy` (whole troops: the stack, the whole camp, garrison plus residents). It is additive. `candidates_hash` is taken over the whole option, so a period's hash now covers `enemy` (new periods only; files of earlier runs have no `enemy` and the page says so). `options_hash` is unchanged by construction (it covers `{option, kind, p, q}` only), so the A/B pair rule is unaffected: asserted in the tests.
   `enemy` depends on script-bot timing like `value` and `own` do, so it is no more deterministic than they are.
2. The page reads three files the contract's §8.3 table already lists but the page did not read: `open/index.json` (written by the release job), `minds/late/<b>.json`, `redactions.json`. No producer changed.
3. Dictionary keys renamed: `council.value` to `council.enemy` (+ `council.enemy_unknown`), `ev.clash_lost_none` to `ev.clash_lost_unknown`; new keys `ev.after`, `ev.fate.*`, `dec.words_autopilot`, `dec.by_unknown`, `dec.remembered_loading`, `dec.not_sent`, `keys.err.ai_key`, `keys.err.noseat` (EN and JA).
4. The Japanese wording of three lines (`dec.remembered`, the new strings) is mine and unreviewed.
5. The AI/script/seat labels and the AI badge are untouched (owner decision pending).

- **The council `result.clash.lost` line** (`calls.mjs` writes `lost` per nation, and `{}` when no before-figure exists): with `{}` the page now says "losses are not in this file" where it said "no troops lost recorded". True either way, and the old wording could read as "zero".
- **Limits of the new clash line.** `troops_after` is the sum of the owner's fighters in the report after the clash (it includes the owner's non-engaged residents in that province); there is no "before" in `events/latest.json`, so the page cannot show losses from this file. `fate` is that of the owner's last engaged or arriving fighter. To show real losses the producer (`events_pub.mjs`, AC6) would have to add `troops_before` and `lost` per actor (`feed.clashDetail` already computes them): a small watcher change, **not made here**.
- **Redaction** reaches the talk feed. A redacted motion's text on the council panel refreshes through the existing 3-second council poll (the service blanks it); I did not test that path. Redacted episodes are refetched by the existing slow poll (10 s). The opened-record texts are not redacted by anything in the contract.
- **Cost of the late probe:** up to 8 GETs per page bell (mostly 404; once per bell, not per 3-second poll). The old open probe asked up to 8 per poll.
- The wave-B plan items outside the page (A1, A2, C1 to C5, D1 to D3, F1 except the boundary guard, G1, G2) belong to other units and were not touched.
