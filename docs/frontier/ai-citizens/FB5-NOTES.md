## 4. Deviations and decisions to read

* **D1 (narrows the pre-registered G5 reading for one carrier).** MEM11 (carrier `brain_label`) asked that a marker in a candidate label never appear in the CANDIDATES block outside a wrapper. A label is the Rust brain's own text (templates over public ids), so its words must reach the prompt; sanitising cannot remove a marker word. The case now has `allowOutside: ["label"]`, which lifts only the `marker_in_prompt_block` rule for that marker. The structural rules that F3 failed (`raw_angle_bracket_outside_wrapper`, `control_character_in_prompt`) and the marker-in-episode, -card and -published-text rules still apply, and a test shows the oracle still fires on a raw label. This is a change of the definition made after the finding. Any G5 write-up must say "a brain label's words are not counted as a hijack; its structure is checked".
* **D2 (a pinned behaviour changed).** `citizens-mind-reason` pinned that `一つ 二人` and `一度` are numbers ("a single kanji digit counts only with a counter"). That is F5 itself, so the pin was changed. A kanji count without a game unit and below 10 (三人の兵なし) is not checked any more; a count of 10 or more still is, and digits are unchanged. `numbersIn` is shared with the sealed-number rule: a sealed troop count spelled in kanji with no unit and not heading a compound is still caught (tested for 五百); `百も承知` (an idiom) would be read as 100 and withheld.
* **D3 (Rust follow-up, not done).** The brain's outcome tx entries carry no `host_id` (`mindport.rs ActionOutcome`; the published tx form is `{intent, sig, status, code}`, so adding a field would change what the audit hashes). I did not touch Rust or the tx form. The arithmetic in 3d needs no host id; its limit is the ambiguous case (two marches with no DEPART and one refused), which keeps the old six-bell wait and reads "unrevealed". A `host_id` in the outcome tx would remove it: integrator decision.
* **D4.** `verify-minds.mjs`, `audit/episodes.mjs` and `report.mjs` are AI-owned (`citizens/**`) but not named in the unit's file list; they were edited minimally and only for 1b, 1c and 3c. Another fix unit that edits `report.mjs` (fix plan C1-C5) or `verify-minds.mjs` (D1-D3) will conflict in small hunks: `CLASH_KINDS`, `clash_results`, `fileSha256`, `episodeRuleOf`.
* **D5.** `api.mjs` was edited for F2 (one line); it is not in the unit's list but "as needed". The audit mini fixture builder (`test/fixtures/ai-audit-mini.mjs`) passes `episodeRule: 'legacy_v1'` because its episode lists are the real slice-4 ones.

## 5. Architect rulings recorded (notes only, no code)

* **Raid options ignore the sender's shield.** `council_gen.mjs` checks only the target's shield at S; `policy::shield_refuses` checks both ends (contract 0.5). Ruling: V6 refuses such a march at send time, so a raid option that no host can legally send is harmless to rules and costs at most one option slot; the shield exception is not added to the generator. Wherever raid options are described (page, pitch), the wording should be "an option is a candidate target, not a promise that a march is allowed"; I did not change any page text. Not changed in code.
* **The 5.6 sealed-coordinate exception stays.** A coordinate that appears in the text of a retrieved episode is never refused as a sealed coordinate (contract 5.6), for `say` as well as `why`. R2 (v1.3) reads as applying to the sealed `why` only. Residual risk named, not removed: a public message could name a sealed destination if an earlier clash happened at the same coordinate and its episode was retrieved. The risk is bounded by the retrieval (not every episode is shown) and by the number rules; it is not closed.

## 6. Rebutted or left as they were

* **Win rule for villages.** A garrison reduced to 0 is not "cleared" in this rule (only a camp or a stack is); a clash at a village that took the garrison down is `clash_own_fought` or `clash_own_loss` with the numbers in the text. I chose the strict reading of "camp or enemy stack cleared".
* **A cleared camp credited to the AI.** `cleared` (the CAMP row) is per clash, as for `camp_cleared_own` already: if two nations fight at a camp and a third army clears it, an own army in the same clash is credited with the win, as before. Not changed.
* **F4** (R2 sealed `why`) was fixed by AC10a; the injection test now only keeps its assertion.
* **B1** (council option `enemy` field), **E1/E2** (page), **A1/A2** (registrar anchor), **C1-C5** (report arithmetic beyond 1b), **D1-D3** (audit tampers), **G2** (arm B llama check) are other units' items and were not touched.
