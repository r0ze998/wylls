Unit AC1b of wave A, AI-CITIZENS-CONTRACT v1.2 (`73408a5`). Branch `frontier/ai-ac1b`, cut from `frontier/ai-ac1a` at `739e337`, local commits only (no push). Everything here ran on this Mac against loopback fakes and files on disk. No llama-server was started (only AC1a's step 0 and the slice gate may), no devnet or mainnet, no paid API or key, no download. Nothing here says the model wants or intends anything: the checks are word lists and arithmetic over code-made facts, not a judge of intent. ## 4. Deviations and choices where the contract is silent

1. **A refused reflection discards the whole answer.** Section 4.7 says the previous summary is kept; it does not say what happens to the answer's goal ops and trust deltas. I apply none of them when the summary is refused (an answer with a refused summary may carry injected content).
2. **Reflection records.** Section 7.2 lists kind `reflection` but nothing else about it. The job writes one record per attempt: accepted gives `mode: "model", reason: "ok"`; a refused summary gives `mode: "autopilot", reason: "invalid:V5b"`; a failed call gives `mode: "autopilot"` with the reason (`timeout`, `llm_error`, `no_time`, `invalid:V0`, `invalid:V1`). They carry an extra field `reflection: {accepted, refused, summary_sha256, goal_ops, trust}`, `wake: ["W-REFLECT"]`, `retrieved` = the window's episode ids, and the stored request body for the M9 replay. M11's "recompute `retrieve()`" does not apply to these (they are not sealed or opened records). Counters: `reflections_run`, `reflection_refused`, `reflection_refused_by{rule}`, `reflection_failed{reason}`, `reflection_skipped{reason}`, `summaries_published`, and the shared `model_decisions`, `decisions_total`, `decisions_model`, `valid_choices`, `decisions_autopilot`, `autopilot_reason`, `fallback_reason`. Because section 10.1 counts a reflection as a model decision, a refused reflection counts against the valid-choice rate unless the report filters by kind; the report (AC9) should state this.
3. **An empty window is skipped** (no model call, no record, counted `reflection_skipped{no_episodes}`): with no episode since the last accepted summary there is nothing to summarise.
4. **The summary window.** Episodes created after the last accepted summary's bell and before the reflection bell, importance x 0.5^(age/72), top 12. The previous summary's text is **not** a grounding source (the contract says the window's episodes); `config.memory.summary_allow_previous: true` adds it (default off, not used by any shipped config).
5. **Count by word.** A refused pact word returns `reason: "pact_word"` and `word: "<stem>"`; `speech.counts()` keeps `{reasons, words}`. The reason string stays plain so the published "(reason withheld by the checker: pact_word)" does not print the word.
6. **Too long is a refusal** (`too_long`), not a cut: the schema already limits a say to 280 and a why to 200, so only an NFKC expansion can exceed it.
7. **Language rule** (V5, `say` only): `ja` needs a kana or kanji and at most half of its letters Latin; `en` needs a Latin letter and no kana, kanji or other script. A direct message uses `ctx.recipientLang` when the mind provides it (AC1a passes the channel language for every message today).
8. **Wider word lists than the contract's examples.** Target-kind words include the tier names of a village (hamlet, town, city, stronghold, holding), barbarian, outpost, settlement; capture objects include lands, provinces, regions, hexes, tiles, realms; human claims include "I am the operator" in several forms and "not a bot"; the abuse list is small and pinned in the file. All lists are exported constants. The pact list is the contract's (betray, pact, treaty, alliance, promise, truce, and "non-aggression") with inflections, plus the five JA words.
9. **Numbers.** Digits, fullwidth digits, thousands separators and kanji numerals (value 10 or more, or a single digit followed by a counter) count; English number words ("six hundred") do not. A digit run directly after a letter (H1, C2, c4) is a handle, not a number. A unit word belongs to the nearest number token and a window never crosses a line break or sentence end; a unit class is troops, bell, hex, province, minute, hour, day, works, stamina, percent, host, wall and the eight resource names. A number the text gives no known unit word only has to occur. Signs are ignored.
10. **`prompt.mjs` no longer calls `speech.sanitize`** (always the real sanitiser); the `speech` argument of `createPromptRenderer` is accepted and unused. AC1a's `speech-stub.mjs` is now dead code once `server.mjs` finds this unit's `speech.mjs`; it names the pact words, so delete it at merge (A.5 grep).

## 5. What the integrator must wire (I may not edit these files)

- **Start the reflection job** (`citizens/server.mjs`, AC1a's): after `const mind = createMind(...)` and before `closer`:
  ```js
  const reflMod = await tryImport('mind/reflection.mjs');
  const reflection = reflMod?.createReflection
    ? reflMod.createReflection({ mind, prompt, stores, views, speech, metrics, records, clock, roster, config, renderPersona, nameOf, nationName, sealed, season, stateDir: STATE })
    : null;
  reflection?.start();   // and reflection?.stop() in close()
  ```
  Without it nothing runs a reflection (the event is emitted and ignored); the rest of memory is unaffected (section 5.3 cut path). `config.memory.reflection:false` also turns it off.
- **Per-word counts** for section 10.1 ("pact_word and uncited-memory-claim withholds by word"): add `speech.counts()` to `mind.metrics()`'s `extra` (or merge `words` into the `speech_drop` group). `applySpeech` only sees `reason`.
- **Sealed entries for speech** (`api.mjs sealedForSpeech`): the checker also reads `nations` (numbers) when present, to refuse "nation 3", "N3", 「国3」; today the mind passes only the nation *names*, which is enough for the names but not for the number forms. Passing `nations: e.nations` is a one-line change.
- **The reflection prompt template** (`prompts/reflection.en.txt`, AC1a's) says every coordinate, bell and number may come from "below" (the ledger digest and goals too), while the validator accepts only the window's episodes (section 4.7). A summary that cites a goal progress or a trust value is refused (`number_not_in_window`). Either change the sentence to "from the episodes below" or accept the refusals; the counter `reflection_refused_by` shows which. Not run against real Gemma, so the refusal rate is unknown.
- **The `why` of a march decision.** `api.mjs` passes the decision's own sealed entries into the `why` check, and section 4.5 forbids target-kind words in any text of an AI with a march in flight, so a `why` such as "clear the camp for Works" is replaced by "(reason withheld by the checker: target_kind)". That is the contract as written (7.2: "a `why` written during flight never names where the army went") and it will make the featured march reason of the demo (section 9.1) mostly code-withheld unless the model words it without place, kind or number. If the owner wants the opened `why` shown, the sealed-set check on `why` needs a different rule; I did not change it.
- Delete `mind/speech-stub.mjs` and drop `allow_speech_stub` at merge.

## 6. Open points and limits

1. **Not run against real Gemma.** The refusal and false-positive rates of the lists on real `say` and `why` are unknown; section 3 gives the only real-text check (407 lab summaries, other prompt). The slice gate or I-A should read `/v1/metrics` (`speech_drop`, `why_withheld`, `reflection_refused_by`) first.
2. **The word lists are not a judge.** Misspellings, spacing tricks, other languages and fluent false claims that avoid numbers and memory words pass (section 4.5 says so). English number words are not numbers. The echo check is verbatim over 24 code points after case and whitespace folding; a paraphrase passes (G5's two-hop case relies on the other rules there).
3. **Number grounding is lexical.** A model that writes a grounded number in a wrong sentence ("500 troops arrive at bell 404" when 404 is a different host's) passes; the unit check narrows this only for known unit words. Two unit words that both sit within the window of a JSON-style fact (`"earliest_bell":404,"hexes":6`) can let "404 hexes" pass.
4. **`ctx.mem` is the list of cited ids.** A decision that cites any episode may then make memory claims in every `say` and in `why`, whether or not the claim concerns the cited line (the contract's rule).
5. **A sealed Call** is treated like a march in flight (target-kind words and identifying numbers refused for a member), as section 4.5 says.
6. **`why` and `say` may hold M-handles** (`M2`): only the summary refuses them; a public `M2` is neutralised when it re-enters a prompt through the hall.
7. **The reflection deadline** needs the clock anchor from a `/v1/decide`; before the first decide a due reflection is skipped with `no_clock` and is not retried (the event fires once per AI and slot).
8. **Direct-message language** is the channel language until the mind passes `recipientLang`.
