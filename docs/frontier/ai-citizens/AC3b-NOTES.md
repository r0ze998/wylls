## 3. Behaviour (what the code does)

**Strike-Order follow (§6.6).** With `--follow-council`, at a bell `b` in `[follow_from, S - 1]` (from the public `GET /f/ai/council?faction=`: `adopted`, `sealed`, `follow_from`, `strike_bell`), a bot reads its sealed Call once (`GET /f/ai/council/call?faction&period&wallet&unix&sig`, signed by the **session key**; `unix` = game time), and if one of its ready combat hosts is on `invited` it fetches the path provinces, plans with `extra = S - earliest - 1` (so the arrival bell is S, else not offered), requires `!shield_refuses`, and sends the Depart (tip by `pick_tip` for script bots, the highest preset for AIs; stance and retreat by `pick_stance` and `pick_retreat` on `Rng::fork(seed, index, bell)`). A bot follows a Call **at most once**. A `NotMember`, not invited, an unreadable Call after 6 bells: nothing more for that period, no re-read. A refused read that may lift (`WindowClosed`, `RateLimited`, 5xx, transport) is retried at later bells.

- **AI citizens** follow inside their per-bell step. The autopilot's Call march is `by: autopilot, via: strike_order`, never a model march (G12), dropped for a reserved host (`standing::filter_follow`) and for a declined period (`follow::intents` checks `declined_calls` itself: AC3a's call site passes no period). Without the mind the fallback keeps the follow (§3.6).
- **The flagged candidate** (§4.3): right after `hold`, `kind: march`, `flags.council: true`, `params.timing: ["call"]`, facts with the key set of the pinned sample plus the reward text of the target kind; the label says what the target is (camp, open-field army, village). Offered only to a member whose invited host is ready, passes the V3(a)-(c) caps and can arrive at S. A model choice of it is `by: model`, `via: strike_order`, counted in the day's caps (V3 b and d) but **not** in `model_marches_sent` and not in the first-model-march timeline (`model_strike_order_marches_sent` instead): the headline "an AI's own march" (G12) leaves the Strike Order out.
- **Script bots** (`follow_wake`, called by the `fleet.rs` hook in every pass of a bot's loop, at most every 5 s real): one attempt per bell, 90 s plus `index mod 60` game seconds into it. The micro-step reads the council state (cached, shared), the member read (once per Call), `/h/me` (is any invited host mine? else `Done("not invited")` and no further wake), and only for a bot with an invited host an observation, the fetch and the Depart. `next_wake` brings the next check forward to the next bell while the window lasts. The counters `follow_window_script_bots` and `follow_window_ai_bots` (in `ai-brain.json`) count bots that actually stepped in a window, once per Call; no present-host count is credited to a bot that did not. **The presenter's seat never follows** (the operator moves it).

**Raid.** One candidate: the nearest other-nation village (province distance from the host, then `(p, q, tile)`) for which `plan_depart` gives a plan, i.e. `shield_refuses` is false at the planned arrival bell for both the target's and the sender's shield. Facts: `target_kind: raid`, `shield: "ended"`, `enemy_troops` (garrison plus other nations' hosts on the tile), the ratio word, the fixed reward text "troops lost only; no land can be taken". Same host as the camp and field marches (`march_host`), so no candidate is offered that V3 would drop.

**Recall.** Step 0 (a) of AC3a found that the program accepts a march onto the own holding tile, so the kind stays. Offered (up to 2, largest first) for a ready combat host standing in **another province** (a host in the home province counts as home troops and has no path to a tile it stands on; a host in transit is never offered: there is no recall of an in-flight march) while **a threat is live** or **the home floor is low**. A threat is a DEPART of another nation's host whose origin is at most 3 provinces from home and whose arrival bell has not passed; the brain reads it from `/h/events` itself (`ThreatFeed`: once per bell per process, pages of 500, at most 20 pages a poll; the owner's nation from the site mirror of the holding the host id names). "Home floor low" is the V3(c) floor already violated: `home_troops * 100 < 40 * H0`, H0 >= 200. Facts as the pinned sample (`threat_origin`, `threat_mass`, `threat_arrive_bell`, `destination: "unknown"`, `home_by_arrival`) plus `earliest_bell`, `hexes`, `provinces`, `reward`; the floor variant has `trigger`, `home_troops`, `home_troops_day_start` instead of the threat keys. A recall is exempt from the V3 caps, is not a march of the day, and the recalled host is reserved for 12 bells (§3.6).

**Signing and posting.** `post_social` takes the answer's `social.say[]`, `social.motion` and `social.ballot` (the shapes AC1a's `api.mjs` produces: numeric channel, `target` faction or base58 wallet, `ref` for a motion = `period*256 + option`, hex `candidates_hash` and `nonce`), fills the brain's wallet, encodes, signs with the session key and POSTs `{bytes_b64, sig_b64, decision_id, item}`. A decision is posted at most once; a refusal is counted by code and never retried (the service consumes `(decision_id, item)` once); an item that fails to encode (over 280 code points, empty, null `candidates_hash`, a motion for option 0) is never signed.

## 6. Deviations from the contract (each also in the final report)

1. **Posting the mind's social items is built here** (`follow::post_social`). §8.1 says the brain signs and POSTs, but neither the AC3a nor the AC3b row names it; `aisign.rs` has no other consumer than the call read.
2. **The brain reads `/h/events` itself for recall.** §4.3 says a recall needs "a W-THREAT live" but the request has no field for it and the mind never builds candidates; the contract does not say how the brain learns of one. `ThreatFeed` is a second reader of the same public records the feed of AC6a reads; it can only offer or withhold a recall.
3. **"Home floor low"** is not defined in the contract; the V3(c) floor already violated (40 % of H0, H0 >= 200) was chosen. A recall is offered only for a host in **another province**.
4. **The flagged candidate and the autopilot follow exist only with `--follow-council`** (§8.4 gives that flag to "script bots and the AI autopilot"). The flagged candidate is offered only for an **invited** host (§4.3 says "an own ready combat host can arrive at S"; an uninvited host is not counted as present, §6.5 step 7) and honours V3(a)-(c) like every march candidate.
5. **A model-chosen Strike-Order march counts in the day's caps but not as a model march** in `model_marches_sent` and the timeline (section 3).
6. **"Replace this step's policy Depart for that host"** (§6.6) is realised for **script bots** as a micro-step that sends first: no hook was added to `Bot::step` for rule bots (the hook budget is AC3a's). A rule Depart of the same host in the same bell would be refused by the program. For AI citizens the rule Departs are dropped by the autopilot filter, so the follow is the only Depart.
7. **The member read is once per bot and Call**, not once per bell (§6.6 estimates "about 6 reads per bot per Call"); the public council state is read once per bell and nation for the process. The micro-step runs once per bell from 90 s plus `index mod 60` s into the bell, inside the loop AC3a's hook already calls (every pass, at most every 5 s real).
8. **The Call's kind is not a fact key** of the flagged candidate (the key set is pinned by the sample): it is in the label and the reward text.
9. **The golden `obs_digest` changed** (section 2) because `ai_wire.rs` now serves the early-run state; the golden candidates are unchanged.
10. **Edits to AC3a's files** (section 2): `follow_offer` removed from `mod.rs`; `brain.rs` extended; one assertion in `ai_brain.rs`; the producer in `ai_wire.rs`.
11. **`ThreatFeed` starts at event 0** and reads at most 20 pages per bell, so a brain started late in a long run catches up over several bells (a run that starts with the stack has no backlog).
12. **`ai-social-v1.json` is a copy** of AC4's file on this branch so the conformance test is self-contained.
