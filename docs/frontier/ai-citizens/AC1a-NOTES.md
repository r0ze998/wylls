## 6. Deviations from the contract (each also in the final report)

1. **max_tokens: reaction 192 to 256, ballot 96 to 160** (section 3: 3 of 8 reactions and 1 of 8 ballots were cut off at the contract values; 36 of 36 are valid at the new ones). Session 384, reflection 512 and motion 160 are as pinned. The bigger caps do not change the call deadlines.
2. **start-pinned.sh flags** are the contract's section 7.1 flags, not the lab script's (section 2).
3. **Stray params**: params for a candidate that was not chosen are dropped and counted (`params_stray_dropped`), not a V2 failure. The grammar cannot tie `params` to `choose` and 4 of 60 measured answers had it. V2's list in 4.5 ("params allowed") does not name the key rule; the 4.4 table does. Strict numbers are reported in section 3.
4. **Record id and sealing**: `id` = sha256 of the canonical published form **without `id` and without `tx`** (the id is the `decision_id` the brain needs before the outcome exists; the leaf of `minds_root` still covers `tx`). A record is sealed when it is added, from the sends it intends (a chosen march or recall candidate, autopilot Strike-Order departs, or a live Strike Order of its nation); it never flips if the outcome later shows nothing was sent. `release_bell` = the arrival bell of the latest march + 1 (the bell index at which the destination is public), or S + 2, whichever is later. The opened record carries extra fields `situation_hash`, `candidates_hash` (needed to recompute the commit), `release_bell`, `index`, `ai`, `bell`, `mode`. `decision_id` is the 64-hex id (the fixture's `d-0123...` is illustrative; the brain treats it as an opaque string).
5. **caps in the answer** are the allowance at the **start** of the decision (fixture: 1,000 home troops, answer with a 500-troop march, `march_troops_left` 600, `home_floor` 400), because the brain's V6 checks the decision's marches against them. Autopilot answers carry null caps (as the fixture).
6. **One user message** with the six sections of 5.5 joined by blank lines (Gemma's chat template alternates roles); the mind prints a `PEOPLE` legend (`C1 Name (nation n)`) because the handles `C1..` are the mind's and AC2's block prints names; the `NOW` line does not show `secs_left` (on the wire it is game seconds to the season end, not seconds left in the bell).
7. **Permission flags**: one `--allow-fs-read=<path>` flag per path. On Node 20.19.4 a comma-separated list with more than one entry made the entry script unreadable (measured), so `permissionFlags()` returns repeated flags; macOS resolves `/tmp` to `/private/tmp`, so callers should pass real paths.
8. **Reflection**: AC1a ships the primitives (the `reflect_due` event at the first step at or after `bell mod reflect_every == 0` for slot >= 1, `llmJob`, `renderReflection`, the schema and its V1) and the 512-token budget; it does not run the reflection job (AC1b's). The trigger consumes no session or reaction budget.
9. **`llmJob` refuses a call with under 500 ms to the deadline** (`no_time`), mirroring the brain's rule.
10. **Day caps** use the ledger's `day_start` (set at the first ready step of each game day from `home_troops_day_start`); a recall is not a march for V3, a Strike-Order-flagged march candidate is.
11. **speech-stub.mjs** exists so the unit runs and is tested without AC1b. It implements the sanitiser of 4.6, length limits, URL/hex/base58/coordinate patterns, sealed coordinates and names, the pinned pact words and human/operator claims (short lists), capture claims and the memory-claim-without-citation rule. It does **not** implement the numbers-in-facts rule, the 24-code-point echo check, the direction and target-kind word lists, the abuse list or the language-script rule. `server.mjs` refuses to start with it unless `allow_speech_stub` is true, and `/v1/health` reports `speech: "stub"`. It names the pact words (the A.5 grep allows the pinned denylist in `mind/speech.mjs` only; AC9's grep should treat `speech-stub.mjs` the same, or the stub should be deleted at merge).

## 8. Open points

1. **Gate state restarts empty**: after a mind restart each AI's `last session bell` is lost, so every AI pulses at its next step (`gate.restore` exists, unused). Ledger counters, records, cached answers and the sealed set are persisted.
2. **`my_clashes` is `[]` on the wire** (AC3a: the files `observe` reads carry no loss figures) and the mind does not fill it; the Remembered lines (`clash_own_*`, `attacked_own` episodes) are the model's source.
3. **A sealed target can reappear as a candidate** at the next step (the brain's list is rebuilt each bell); the mind does not remove candidates (section 5.5: never candidates) and instead refuses the sealed coordinates in model text (V5 via the sealed set, stored entries plus this decision's own).
4. Synchronised pulses: AIs join one bell apart, so their 24-bell pulses start staggered, but a clash or a Strike Order wakes many at once; at 6.1 s per session about 9 fit in a 60 s bell on a quiet Mac, so `no_time` drops are expected when 12-18 AIs wake together (admission handles it; the report counts them).
5. `W-QUEUE` and `W-READY` come only from the brain's `wake_hints`; if the brain stops sending them nothing else fires them.
6. If the integrator wants candidate `facts` shown in full (the renderer hides `host_id`, `ratio_is`, `tile`, `not applicable`, values already in the label, and names resource costs), change `prompt.mjs factText`.
7. The contract's `T-K1` is the brain's; AC1a's part of the key-isolation claim is T-K2 and T-K4.
