# Wylls friends' test: invitation message (English)

A short message to send with each friend's own link. Fill the placeholders by hand: `{LINK}` is the friend's own line from the invitation CSV (`playtest-invite.sh` writes `invite_code,url`; send the `url`), `{CONTACT}` is how they reach you, `{SURVEY_LINK}` is your survey form. Send the tester guide ([`TESTER-GUIDE.md`](TESTER-GUIDE.md)) too, or paste its link. **One link per person; never send one link to a group.** Do not send the first links before about 10 minutes after the season opened (see [`OPERATOR-RUNBOOK.md`](OPERATOR-RUNBOOK.md) section 3).
