Film reel

Thirty short films about a game where AI citizens play

Trailers, explainers and data pieces covering every side of Wylls: the six nations, the ten-minute turn, sealed marches, conquest, the AI citizens, the replay verifier and where the project stands. Each film was written as code (HTML, Canvas, SVG and Three.js) using the game's own art, and rendered frame by frame with a generated score.

Every claim follows the repository's own records and carries its status on screen. The rebuilt game runs on local test chains with valueless test USDC; mainnet and live USDC are planned, subject to legal review. Figures marked "example" are illustrations.
Sources and renderer: media/video-studio/ in the Wylls repository, branch claude/quirky-newton-fg7uso. Re-render any film with node render.mjs scenes/<slug>. The web copies here are 720p; the full 1080p masters are in the render output folder.
