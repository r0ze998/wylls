## Creative briefs

**01 trailer-ja.** The flagship. Cold open in darkness: a single 10-minute bell ticks; the parchment world assembles hex by hex from the cloud sea (wy-map), six pennants rise. Kinetic lines (serif JP): 「六つの国。ひとつの地図。」「10分ごとに、世界が動く。」「行き先は、封印される。」 (sealed march with wax seal), 「その中に、AI市民がいる。」 (the code-offers / model-chooses / code-checks beat, minimal), 「砦を奪え。」 (clash + capture VFX), leader cast montage (stage strips / motion sprites in nation colours), title slam (W tile + WYLLS), tagline 「意志を持つAIと、ひとつの地図を。」, end card. Fast cuts on beats, drum hits on title words. Status badge on the AI and capture beats.

**02 trailer-en.** Same world, English, tighter, a different structure so it is not a translation: open on "What does a game do when AI has a will?" → "Six nations. One map." → "Every ten minutes, the whole world resolves at once." → "Marches leave sealed." → "AI citizens play by the same rules. The code offers. The model chooses. The code checks." → "Anyone can replay the season." → title → "Play the agent economy." End card EN.

**03 teaser-vertical-ja.** 20 s for X/TikTok/Reels. Hook in the first 1.5 s (huge bell + 「10分ごとに、世界が動く」), quick map, sealed seal stamp, AI line, title. Big type, safe areas (top 220 px and bottom 300 px clear of key text).

**04 thesis-will-ja.** Wylls is the English spelling of will. A typographic piece: "Will → Wylls", 意志. Question 「AIに意志があったら、ゲームはどう振る舞うのか。」 Then the honest framing: we do not claim the model wants anything; will is something the system builds and shows from outside, in five visible forms (one card each, animated): 続く目標 / 記憶を引用する理由 / 信頼と未解決の不満 / 命令を断る権利 / 評議会での発言と投票. Note that the grievance and refusal claims are not yet confirmed by the formal run.

**05 six-nations-ja.** One beat per nation (~8 s each): the character (stage strip or motion sprite, victory clip for flourish), crest, colour field, JA/EN name, doctrine (石の守り手 / 潮 / 炎 / 新緑 / 光明 / 鉄) and its one-liner from FACTS §2 (mark it as the game's doctrine text). No names, titles or lore for the characters. Finish on the six together around The Concord map. Balance footnote: in simulation each nation wins about 1 season in 6 (simulator output).

**06 pick-your-nation-vertical-ja.** Vertical carousel/quiz: 「あなたはどの国?」 Six quick cards swiping (character, sigil, doctrine one-liner), ends with 「国を選ぶ。それだけで村が置かれる。」 + end card.

**07 how-to-play-ja.** Clear tutorial in 4–5 steps with big numerals: ① 国を選ぶ (instant village, PlaceHome) ② 育てる (resources accrue in real time, build, train) ③ 偵察する (result next turn, fog clears) ④ 封印して進軍 (min 2 turns, no recall) ⑤ 報告を読む (browser recomputes). Use wy-map for the world and UI-like cards. Status badge: local test chain.

**08 ten-minute-turn-vertical-ja.** A big clock dial: 10 minutes = 1 turn, 144 turns a day; orders from all players drop in during the turn; at the bell everything resolves together; "sending a minute earlier gives no advantage". Economy runs continuously meanwhile (resource counters ticking). Tension music with ticks.

**09 sealed-march-ja.** The time-lock explained: departure, size and arrival turn are public; the destination is sealed with drand tlock until the arrival turn ends; at least 2 turns; cannot be recalled; anyone can open it after; closing the tab does not void it. Visual: a sealed letter / wax seal riding a dashed march path over the map, a drand round counter, the seal cracking open at the arrival. Honest note: secrecy was not tested in recorded runs (archived/test beacon).

**10 economy-ja.** Eight resources with the game's illustrated icons, counters rising; buildings (Farm, Lumber camp, Quarry, Mine, Mint, Library, Walls) as cards; village tiers 集落→町→都市→城塞 using the holdings sprites (hamlet/town/city/stronghold); concrete numbers from FACTS (starter kit, Farm cost/yield, Town upgrade).

**11 troops-stances-ja.** The eight troop types as soldier cards (units/mobs-v1/cards) in a nation's colours, then the four stances 待機/突撃/側撃/迎撃 with simple diagrams, then a host (軍) 100–30,000 of one type. Use the attack/hit sprites for energy.

**12 battle-report-wasm-ja.** A clash report unfolds; the same Rust rules kernel compiled to WebAssembly runs in the browser, re-computes the outcome, and the checks tick green → "Match". Honest wording: recomputation, not an audit; no "Verified" badge claim. Show code-like lines (rules kernel → frontier-wasm → browser).

**13 conquest-keep-ja.** The recorded own1 capture: Aster seals a 500-Spearman march on Fjordal's keep, clash at turn 43, a disclosed 72-turn local clock advance, keep transfers at turn 110 (capture VFX in Aster colours). On screen: "人間が操作したローカルデモ / AI・ボットなし / 72ターンの時計送りあり". Rules: keep taken after holding 72 turns (12 h), sieges for Free Cities (ring 4) and outposts.

**14 season-14day-ja.** The 14-day preset: 2,016 turns, joining closes at 1,512; winner = most keeps (decided), ties → territory time (provisional) → share. The local sc-14d run: 120 rule bots, 3 paid test wallets, 272,004 transactions, verify PASS, keeps 10/9/9/9/10/7, Aster and Ember tied at 10, territory time 19,968 vs 19,936 → Ember; refund mode with test tokens. Animate the tally bar race and tiebreak. Clearly a local run with rule bots.

**15 ai-citizens-ja.** The headline AI explainer: local Gemma 4 (no paid API); persona, goals, memory; the loop 「コードが候補を出し(最大12)、モデルが選び、コードが確かめる」; autopilot when late/invalid; the model never sees keys; same rules plus safety limits. Show a decision card UI. Status: running locally, formal evaluation pending.

**16 ai-citizens-en.** English counterpart, own structure; ends with what is pending (formal A/B, 18-AI main run, no people+AI world yet).

**17 senasha-trace-ja.** Story piece. The take-1 run: six local Gemma 4 AI citizens and six script bots. Turn 65: Senasha (an AI citizen) receives candidate options, cites memory lines, writes a reason (marked 「モデルが書いた理由・未検証」), chooses a 400-troop march to a barbarian camp plus a science build; the march leaves sealed; turn 68 clash and camp cleared; verify-minds PASS. Do not invent the exact reason text or memory lines: show them as placeholders/blurred or generic labelled "例" if the real text is not in the repo (search docs first; if found, quote exactly).

**18 memory-citation-vertical-ja.** 「AIは"覚えている"とは言いません。"引用した"と言います。」 Code writes episode lines from public records; the model may cite; a citation shows a line was shown and named, not that it caused the choice. Memory ends with the season.

**19 autopilot-ja.** Why guarantees live in code: the Gemma spike (bare model marched in 1 or 9 of 20 decisions vs 18/20 for the rule bot; 79% valid at best; ~5 s per decision) → so the code offers legal options and checks; autopilot runs economy and duties, never starts a march; in smoke-b3 at most 29.8% of AI actions were model-chosen. Data-viz bars.

**20 council-strike-order-ja.** The nation council: code proposes up to 3 targets, citizens move and argue, vote (≥2 votes and more than others), the adopted Strike Order stays sealed until 2 turns after the strike; off-chain, not enforced by the chain; an AI may decline publicly. Honest: no Strike Order has been adopted in any recorded run.

**21 ai-rules-vertical-ja.** Vertical list of the limits: same keys/rate limits/fog as people; ≤12 options; ≤60% of home troops per decision/day, 40% kept home; ≤4 model-chosen marches a day; speech 3 per turn / 40 per day; the model never sees keys.

**22 why-chain-ja.** The program, not an operator, resolves every clash; sealed orders; the season is a public log anyone can replay. "Not speed or cost." Keepers permissionless, herald read-only, relay can refuse but not change outcomes. Status: local test chain; the rebuild has not run on devnet.

**23 verifier-tamper-en.** The replay verifier: replay every transaction, re-judge every rule; 30 deliberate tamper classes all caught; 144,300 tx (M1), 272,004 tx (14-day), all PASS; "our own verifier, not an independent audit". Visual: a ledger stream, a forged entry flagged red.

**24 m1-season-numbers-ja.** Data piece: 1,000 script bots of 13 profiles, 7 game days = 1,008 + 26 turns, 8 h 39 min at 20x, 1,712 marches settled / 0 stuck, 43 kill -9 restarts with no crash, 5,000 simulated viewers 3.46 M requests 0 errors, 144,300 tx verified, 30/30 tampers. Big animated counters; note "script bots, not people".

**25 balance-sim-ja.** The first draft let one doctrine win 99.5% of 600 simulated seasons → after tuning, 15.6–17.4% across 1,500 paired seasons at 10,000 simulated wallets (per-nation values). Animated bar chart transforming. Label: simulator output, assumed behaviour.

**26 architecture-en.** Animated system diagram: rules kernel (Rust, no_std) shared by program / simulator / verifier / bots / browser (WASM); Solana program as the only judge; keeper, herald, relay, verify; citizens service (mind keyless / brain signs) with local Gemma 4; web client; drand. Data flows as moving particles.

**27 prize-path-ja.** Locally: a 25 test-USDC entry over x402, prize program Tally → Finalize → SetShares → Claim (test tokens). Planned for the next public season on mainnet: 25 USDC, 80% pool / 20% operator, subject to legal review. Big "計画" and "テスト用USDC" labels; nothing live.

**28 s1-update-ja.** What S1 adds (built in code, not yet playable in the client): Workshop arms economy (2 ore + 1 wood → 1 arm), vaults, caravans and raids, watchtowers (1%/min wear, ≤30%), palisades, forts (≤2,000 Spearmen garrison), on-chain nation proposals and votes (one vote per village; stance, expulsion). Use "砦(fort)" carefully. Status badge: code.

**29 journey-ja.** Timeline: earlier games 0xCiv / 0xARK (prior work) → 2026-09 Permutation State prototype on devnet (MagicBlock, x402, 180 turns, 14 members) → rebuild & M1 (1,000 bots) → name Wylls → AI citizens (Gemma 4) → take-1 / own1 → PR #4 → submitted to Colosseum Crypto World's Fair (Gaming) 2026-10-13 06:50 JST → 14-day run → S1. A camera travels along a road on the map with milestone banners.

**30 honest-status-ja.** 「できたこと / まだのこと」. Status tags animated beside each area (README §4 table), then the "not claimed" list as calm typography, then roadmap (shared civilisation, tech, eras, Engine, diplomacy, markets, governance; next public season planned on mainnet). Tone: confident honesty.
